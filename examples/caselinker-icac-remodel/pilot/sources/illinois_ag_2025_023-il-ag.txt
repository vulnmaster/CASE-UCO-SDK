Title: Attorney General Raoul Obtains 10-Year Prison Sentence for Greene
County Man Who Possessed Child Sexual Abuse Material

Chicago — Attorney General Kwame Raoul announced that a Roodhouse,
Illinois man was sentenced to 10 years in prison for possessing child
sexual abuse material.

Ethan T. Seaton, 20, was sentenced Tuesday by Greene County Circuit Court
Judge Zachary Schmidt after previously pleading guilty to two counts of
Class 2 felony possession of child pornography.

Raoul's investigators, along with officers from the Roodhouse Police
Department and the Illinois State Police South Central Illinois Drug Task
Force, conducted a search of Seaton's residence in Roodhouse in September
2024. He was arrested after admitting to possessing child sexual abuse
materials.

Raoul's office prosecuted the case with the assistance of the Greene
County state's attorney's office.
