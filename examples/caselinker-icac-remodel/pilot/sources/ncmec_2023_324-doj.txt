Title: First Grade Teacher Charged with Solicitation of Child Sexual
Abuse Material

EAST ST. LOUIS, Ill. — A man who taught first grade at New Baden
Elementary School and coached girls' sports teams for Wesclin Community
Unit School District 3 is facing a federal charge accusing him of
soliciting child sexual abuse material.

Jonathan C. Villmer, Jr., 25, of New Baden, Illinois, was charged via
criminal complaint and is scheduled for arraignment in U.S. District
Court in the Southern District of Illinois.

The case was initiated by an otherwise unrelated investigation by the
Carmi Police Department regarding the distribution of CSAM. That
investigation led to an account soliciting CSAM on Snapchat that law
enforcement connected to Villmer.

After obtaining a search warrant for Villmer's Snapchat account, police
discovered explicit conversations with a 13-year-old minor and requests
for images. Sexually explicit conversations with other purported minors
were also identified. IP address records associated with the Snapchat
account connected the account to Villmer. On August 18, 2023, law
enforcement executed a search warrant on Villmer's New Baden residence
and seized two cell phones and other electronic devices. Villmer was
arrested on scene.

A defendant is presumed innocent until proved guilty. If convicted,
Villmer could face up to 20 years' imprisonment.

The Department of Homeland Security Investigations is conducting the
investigation with support from the New Baden and Carmi Police
Departments and the Jefferson County and Clinton County Sheriffs'
Departments. Assistant U.S. Attorney Ali Burns is prosecuting.
