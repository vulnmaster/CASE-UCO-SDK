Title: Mountain View Resident Charged with Production of Child Pornography
and Related Offenses

SAN JOSE — Grant Ridder was arraigned on federal charges of production of
child pornography, distribution and possession of child pornography,
cyberstalking, and coercion and enticement of a minor. The arraignment
follows an indictment handed down by a federal grand jury on May 18, 2017.

Ridder, 26, is a resident of Mountain View. The investigation began after
a 16-year-old reported unauthorized access to a Facebook account and
posting of explicit photographs. A search warrant identifies a second
minor victim, age 13. Ridder was arrested and remanded to the U.S. Marshal
on May 24, 2017.

In the indictment, Ridder is charged with:
- two counts of production of child pornography, 18 U.S.C. § 2251(a);
- three counts of distribution of child pornography, 18 U.S.C. § 2252(a)(2);
- one count of possession of child pornography, 18 U.S.C. § 2252(a)(4)(B);
- three counts of cyberstalking, 18 U.S.C. § 2261A;
- one count of coercion and enticement of a minor, 18 U.S.C. § 2422(b).

An indictment is an accusation. The defendant is presumed innocent.

If convicted of 18 U.S.C. § 2251(a): mandatory minimum 15 years, maximum
30 years, and a $250,000 fine plus restitution if appropriate.
If convicted of 18 U.S.C. § 2252(a)(2): mandatory minimum 5 years, maximum
20 years, and a $250,000 fine.
If convicted of 18 U.S.C. § 2252(a)(4)(B): maximum 10 years and a $250,000
fine.
If convicted of 18 U.S.C. § 2261A(2): maximum 5 years and a $250,000 fine.
If convicted of 18 U.S.C. § 2422(b): mandatory minimum 10 years, maximum
life, and a $250,000 fine.

Investigated by the United States Secret Service, the Contra Costa
District Attorney's Office, the Stockton Police Department, and the
Martinez Police Department. Assistant U.S. Attorney Maia Perez is
prosecuting.
