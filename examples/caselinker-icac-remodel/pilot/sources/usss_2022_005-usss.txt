Title: East Peoria Man Sentenced to 151 Months in Prison for Possession of
Child Sexual Abuse Material

PEORIA, Ill. — Noah Joseph Smith, 37, of East Peoria, Illinois, was
sentenced on March 23, 2022, to 12 years and seven months in prison for
possession of child pornography, to be followed by a life term of
supervised release. No fine was imposed. Smith is required to pay $23,000
in restitution.

A U.S. Probation Officer discovered an unreported LG smart phone during a
March 2021 home visit while Smith was on supervised release for a prior
conviction for receipt of child pornography. An investigation found that
Smith possessed numerous videos and images of child pornography.

Judge James E. Shadid noted a 2007 conviction for aggravated criminal
sexual abuse and a 2008 conviction for receipt of child pornography.
Smith had begun supervised release in that case only months before the
2021 arrest.

Smith was indicted for possession of child pornography in April 2021 and
pleaded guilty in September 2021.

Smith also admitted supervised-release violations and was sentenced to
24 months in the custody of the Bureau of Prisons, concurrent with the
instant case.

Statutory penalties for possession of child pornography, given a
qualifying prior conviction, are a mandatory 10 to 20 years imprisonment;
a maximum $250,000 fine; a maximum lifetime term of supervised release;
a mandatory $100 special assessment or a $5,000 special assessment under
18 U.S.C. § 3014 if not indigent; and a $17,000 special assessment under
18 U.S.C. § 2259A.

The United States Probation Office, United States Secret Service, and
Peoria County Sheriff's Office investigated. Assistant U.S. Attorney
Keith Hollingshead-Cook prosecuted. The case was brought as part of
Project Safe Childhood.
